Hardware Spec
Target Controller

Sensor Selection
Capacitive vs. Resistive

Recommend Capacitive v1.2 probes over resistive forks to eliminate probe corrosion from electrolysis.

Hysteresis Logic
Debounced Control

Avoid rapid relay chatter by introducing a 5-minute cooldown timer between pumping intervals.
